Review in your browser: `hunk diff --web` (also `show`, `stash show`, `patch`, and `difftool`) opens the review in a local page with the terminal's stream, keys, and themes, and `hunk session open` or **File → Open in browser** opens a running terminal review there. The page adds mouse line-range notes, a command palette, go to file, viewed files with progress, an overview and notes panel, a minimap, image comparisons, and rendered Markdown, and it is served only on your machine's loopback address, so nothing leaves it.
